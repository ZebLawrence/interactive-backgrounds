# Hangar: E1M1 trace references

`hangar/html/data/e1m1.json` is generated by `node hangar/tools/build-e1m1.mjs`. The
polygons in that script were traced by hand, in pixels, off published top-down maps of
E1M1. No WAD file was downloaded, opened or parsed, and no id Software art is used. The
map images and screenshots below were only looked at for the trace. They are not in the repo.

**Player start:** Doom (1056, −3616), facing north (angle 90). This is the usual published
value for E1M1's player-1 start. It comes from memory and was not confirmed against a
fetched page. It is used only to anchor the trace in plausible Doom coordinates. The plan's
shape does not depend on it.

**Heights:** I found no published per-sector height table (the web search for one came up
empty). Floor and ceiling heights were therefore set from the screenshots and walkthroughs:
the zig-zag walkway sits above the slime, the courtyard is a step below the start room, the
armour post is raised up a stair, and so on. They were then adjusted for the spec as listed
below. Relative heights follow the original. Absolute values are my own.

## Scale

The trace is 4 Doom units per map pixel, which makes 0.12 m per pixel at `unit = 0.03`. The
original scale of the image is not published. Door openings on the map suggest about
4.3 units per pixel (a 128-unit door spans about 30 px). Room proportions against the
screenshots suggest about 3.6. I picked 4, which keeps the narrowest walkable openings at
about 3 m for a walking camera. The whole level is about 113 m × 74 m, plus the exit view.

## Where the trace was widened, heightened or changed, and why

1. **Slime hall made taller.** The slime is at −48, the walkway at −16 and the ceiling at
   320, so the hall is 11 m from slime to ceiling. In the original it is a modest room. The
   spec asks for "ceilings that breathe": tall halls after low corridors. Corridors stay at
   80 units (2.4 m), so the squeeze-then-release ratio is 4.6×.
2. **Courtyard walls raised.** Courtyard sectors have a sky "ceiling" of 400, so the base
   walls rise about 12.5 m round the open sky (spec: scale through courtyards). The exit
   view's sky is at 520.
3. **Start room: raised platform and stair.** The start spot sits on a platform at +24, with
   a three-tread stair (12) down to the main floor (0). The blue floor is a sunken pit (−16)
   between the four columns. This follows the spec's "raised platform with steps down into a
   wider space". The original start area is close to flat.
4. **Zig-zag hall window.** The map draws a two-sided line on the hall's west wall, and the
   screenshot shows the outdoors through it. It is kept as a glazed window (`zz-window`)
   onto the courtyard (spec: more openings to the sky).
5. **Courtyard slime channel.** The original pool is closed. Here it gets a channel running
   north (under the computer-room window) and south to the courtyard wall. That gives the
   spec's "slime channel", and lets the courtyard be built from simple polygons without holes.
   The triangular area at the courtyard's south-east, which the map separates with a
   two-sided line, is a raised platform (+24) (spec: "a raised platform").
6. **The way out.** In the original, a plain door leads from the dark room to the exit room.
   Here the passage is a **lift** (from −32 up to +104), then a door, then a **raised exit
   room** with a wide window. Beyond the window is a new outdoor sector, `exit-view`, whose
   ground drops to −160 (spec: "a lift or stair up to a high exit room with the widest
   view"). The exit door is widened to the full passage width (about 3.5 m). In the original
   it is about half that.
7. **Chamfers and angles.** The spec's "angles everywhere" (and the test: every walkable
   sector over 20 m² has a wall more than 5° off the axes) led to these changes. The
   computer room has 45° chamfers at its north and east corners. The dark room has bottom
   chamfers. The dark-room dais is chamfered. The exit room has chamfered upper corners. The
   computer-room window is a bay with angled cheeks. The south corridor's mouth onto the
   walkway is angled about 8°.
8. **Computer room simplified.** The narrow back passage round the computer room ("behind
   the computer", where a shotgun guy hides) is left out. The room is split into `comp-main`
   (floor 0) and `comp-core` (+16, round the original U-shaped computer). The console banks
   and the U-shaped computer become props in a later task, not sectors. The window onto the
   courtyard is a glazed sill sector (sill +40, top +160).
9. **Secrets.** Secret #1 is kept: the differently coloured wall in the south corridor
   (`door-tunnel`), the stairs down, the tunnel, and the stairs up into the courtyard. Also
   kept is the Ultimate Doom door from the start room to the courtyard (`door-yard`, F on
   the map). Secrets #2 and #3 are left out: the shotgun alcove (H), the lift (J) and the
   winding passage. The imp ledge on the hall's east side is kept as a raised ledge (+48)
   that the camera cannot reach. It still reads from the walkway.
10. **West armour post.** The outdoor area west of the armour post is a ring of regolith with
    a slime pit inside it. The map's grey inner trapezoid is read as the edge of the pit. The
    armour alcove's three glazed openings (north, south, west) are window sectors. The two
    pillars by the armour stair are props (with lamps), not sectors.
11. **Doors** store their open height as `ceil` (ruling R1). They become animated panels
    later, not moving ceilings.
12. **Approximations.** The curved hallway, the zig-zag band and the dark-room outline follow
    the map's lines to within a few pixels (about ±0.5 m). Where two sources disagreed on
    small notches, the simpler outline was used.
13. **Courtyard platform gets a step (Ruling R9).** The +24 platform stood 1.2 m above the
    courtyard floor with no way up. A one-tread sector `yard-step` (floor +4, 1.1 m deep) now
    runs along its yard-side edge, so both risers are 0.6 m (under the 0.72 m step limit) and
    the platform itself is a little shorter. Vertices are shared exactly with `yard-east`.
14. **Low ceilings raised at portals (Ruling R8).** Headroom through a doorway is the physical
    opening, min(ceil) − max(floor), and must be at least 2.0 m. `hall-b` ceiling 80 → 96 (it
    met `hall-a` at 1.92 m) and `hall-c` ceiling 64 → 80 (it met `hall-b` at 1.92 m).
15. **Courtyard views turned to Mars (Task 10, Ruling R21).** Mars hangs about 25° up over the
    courtyard's open south side (`phobos.marsDirection`), so the three courtyard pois now look
    south and up instead of north at the buildings. `yard-sky` looks from the middle over the
    south parapet (target pixel 708, 400 at height 250). `yard-pool` moved from the south-west
    corner to the east bank by the computer-room window (pixel 600, 180) and looks down the
    slime channel, so Mars and its reflection share the frame (target 585, 330 at 200).
    `yard-platform` looks from the raised platform out over the parapet (target 698, 440 at 185).
    The Doom map has no such views. This is a dressing choice for the payoff view, not part
    of the trace.
