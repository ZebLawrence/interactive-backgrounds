Interactive Backgrounds

Animated backgrounds that react to the mouse.

  • FishbowlA 3D aquarium: fish school, flee a fast cursor, come to inspect a resting one and race for the food you drop in.
  • Firefly MeadowA 3D meadow at night: grass parts around the cursor, fireflies scatter from a fast one, gather round a resting one and fall into step, and a clap sets them all off.
  • Balloon DawnA 3D balloon fiesta over a misty valley at sunrise: your cursor is the wind, a click fires a burner, and at night the balloons glow like lanterns.
  • Loon LakeA 3D mountain lake at dusk, reflected in simulated water: your fingertip stirs the surface, trout and dragonflies come to it when it rests, a click skips a stone, and at night the moon and the Milky Way lie on the lake.
  • Boreal NightA snowy spruce clearing under the northern lights: a great grey owl watches your cursor and plunges into the snow for the vole you click, and a fast cursor is a gust that shakes snow from the trees.
  • Aspen SnowA quaking-aspen grove in falling snow, at eye level among the trunks: snowshoe hares come to look at a resting cursor and bolt from a fast one, a click on the snow flushes a ruffed grouse from its roost under the snow, and a click on a trunk brings its snow down in a cascade.
  • Sandstone ArchA natural bridge over a still lake at night under the aurora, with a weathered craft exploring the canyon beneath it: a fingertip stirs the water, a click tosses a stone or rouses the craft, the craft turns its red eye on a resting cursor, and rain mode brings a storm sky with lightning.
